讀懂加熱曲線Reading a heating curve

用功率 P 的電熱器加熱質量 m 的物質（沒有熱量散失）：Heating a mass m with a heater of power P (no heat loss):

斜率 slope = ΔT/Δt = P / (mc)斜率越大（越斜） → mc 越小 → 熱容量越小。同質量同功率時，越斜 = c 越小。A steeper slope → smaller mc → smaller heat capacity. Same m and P: steeper = smaller c.
平坦段長度 = mℓ / P物態改變時溫度不變。平坦段越長 → mℓ 越大。同質量同功率時，越長 = ℓ 越大。Temperature is constant during a change of state. A longer flat part → larger mℓ. Same m and P: longer = larger ℓ.
Q–ΔT 圖斜率 = C如果 y 軸是能量、x 軸是溫度變化，斜率就是熱容量 C = mc。注意坐標軸對調時意思會反過來！If energy is on the y-axis and temperature change on the x-axis, the slope is the heat capacity C = mc. Swapping the axes reverses the meaning!

實驗誤差：計算值偏大還是偏小？Experimental errors: is the result too large or too small?

🧭 解題方法🧭 How to tackle these

  1. 寫出計算公式，例如 ℓv = Pt / m。Write down the formula used, e.g. ℓv = Pt / m.
  2. 想想這個處理令哪個量度值變大或變小（例如 m 變小）。Decide which measured quantity becomes larger or smaller (e.g. m smaller).
  3. 放回公式：在分子 → 同方向；在分母 → 反方向。Put it back into the formula: in the numerator → same direction; in the denominator → opposite direction.
⭐ 常見規律：用電熱器的實驗，如果有熱量散失，計算時用的能量 Pt 比物質真正吸收的多，所以算出來的 c 或 ℓ 通常偏大。 ⭐ Common pattern: in heater experiments, if heat is lost, the energy Pt used in the calculation is more than the substance really absorbed, so the calculated c or ℓ is usually too large.
